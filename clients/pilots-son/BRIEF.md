# BRIEF — THE PILOT'S SON APPAREL CO.

## Goal
A scroll-animated, cinematic e-commerce site for custom apparel: sunglasses, tees,
hoodies, socks, sweats. Must read as Godly/Awwwards Site-of-the-Day quality AND
convert — this is a shop, not a portfolio piece.

- Sunglasses + socks + sweats added to the range
- A real CUSTOMIZER (the "custom" in custom apparel) — configure and see it live

## Non-negotiables

- Full-bleed interactive video hero
- Jumbo display typography
- Real commercial surfaces: PDP, cart, size, price, add-to-cart, email capture
