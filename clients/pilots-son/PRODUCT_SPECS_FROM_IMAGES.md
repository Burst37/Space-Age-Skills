# FLAGS — things the images show that the site gets wrong

1. **Card 09 "Chrome Autobot" shows a Decepticon.** The insignia on
   `autobot-chrome.webp` is the purple Decepticon faceplate. Autobot and
   Decepticon are opposing factions — anyone who knows the property will spot
   it. Card 19 "Autobot Grey" genuinely is an Autobot. Likely a swapped name.

2. **Cards 08 and 16 are the same garment.** `ufo-notthesame.webp` and
   `prism-crystal.webp` are the identical prism artwork on the identical cream
   crewneck, listed twice at $92 and $88. One of them is wrong, or one is
   missing its real image.

3. **Card 07 "Invasion" has no UFO.** The filename is `ufo-invasion-teal.webp`
   but the piece is a purple Decepticon crewneck. My tagline for it —
   "Arrival from above. Ask who taught them to fly." — describes a UFO. Wrong
   copy on the wrong garment.

4. **Card 12 "Cyber Geisha" is a long-sleeve tee**, not a crewneck, and it is
   priced highest in the drop at $96. The asset also has a dark grid background
   baked into it rather than being cut out, so it renders differently from the
   other eighteen.

5. **Gear intro contradicts the sweater.** The line "the pieces that carry the
   mark without the print" is mine, and item 04 is a full all-over print. Wrong
   either way — it should go or be rewritten by you.

6. **Two assets are artwork, not product shots** — Autobot Grey and the iPhone
   case. Both render as floating graphics on their cards.

7. **Slides have no size run**, so they currently add to cart as "One size."
