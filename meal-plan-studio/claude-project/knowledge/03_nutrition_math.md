# Nutrition math

## Energy
- BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor): women 10 x kg + 6.25 x cm - 5 x age - 161. Men: same with +5.
- TDEE: start at BMR x 1.2 (desk-based, no training). Add the real activity: steps (about 0.04 kcal per step per kg of body weight, roughly 300 to 400 kcal for 10,000 steps) and training (3 hours a week of hard resistance and plyometrics is about 150 to 200 kcal a day averaged). Cross-check with BMR x 1.4 to 1.6. Real intake is ±10%; plan to review after 3 to 4 weekly weigh-ins.
- Loss rate: 0.5% to 1% of body weight a week is the safe range (1 lb of fat is about 3,500 kcal). Daily deficit = lb per week x 3,500 / 7. Never plan below the calorie floor (about 1,200 women, 1,500 men) without supervision.
- Goal date check: weeks needed = pounds to lose / planned lb per week.

## Day-type calories
Weekly average = TDEE minus the deficit. Training days sit about 150 to 200 kcal above the average, rest days about 100 kcal below.

## Protein, carbs, fat
- Protein: 1.6 to 2.4 g per kg actual weight (high end when cutting hard with heavy training), minimum 120 g on keto days if kidneys are healthy.
- Carbs by day type: training days as capped by the client (for example 90 to 98 g total, never above 100 g), low-carb days about 40 to 50 g, keto days net 20 g or less.
- Fat is the remainder, with a floor of about 0.6 g per kg. Keto days run higher in fat on purpose.
- Fiber: 14 g per 1,000 kcal, about 20 to 30 g. Water: 2.5 to 3 L.
- Net carbs = carbs minus fiber.

## Worked example (Aleena, 35, 5'2", 145 lb, goal 125 lb in 4 months, 3 hard sessions, 10k steps)
BMR 1,306. TDEE about 2,000. 20 lb in about 17.4 weeks is 1.15 lb a week, a deficit of 575 kcal. Planned weekly average 1,461 kcal (deficit about 540, about 1.1 lb a week, 19 to 20 lb). Training days 1,543 kcal, 157 g protein, 95 g carbs (never above 98 g). Low-carb days 1,375 kcal, 156 g protein, 46 g carbs. Keto days 1,405 kcal, 127 g protein, 30 g carbs (17 g net), 89 g fat. Protein about 2.2 g per kg.
