# Research playbook: what to check for each client, and the safety gates

Use the sections that match the client's diet, religion, conditions and medications. For each "Check" line, verify with live sources when you have search tools (PubMed, Consensus, society guidelines, FDA/NIH fact sheets) and record: finding, source, effect on this plan. If you cannot verify, write "confirm with physician". Never invent a dose, a timing rule or a citation.

## A. Safety gates (stop and hand back to the dietitian if any is true)
1. Pregnant or breastfeeding: no deficit, no keto, no fasting. Needs a clinician-led plan.
2. Under 18: growth needs; not a weight-loss deck.
3. History of an eating disorder, or restriction that sounds disordered: no calorie targets, no fasting, no keto; refer.
4. Type 1 diabetes, or insulin / sulfonylurea use: low carb, keto and fasting can cause hypoglycemia or ketoacidosis. Physician sign-off and medication plan first.
5. Kidney disease (eGFR reduced): protein, potassium and phosphorus caps set by the nephrologist. Do not run a high-protein plan.
6. Calories below the floor (about 1,200 kcal for women, 1,500 for men) without medical supervision: raise the target or lengthen the timeline.
7. Pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, fat-metabolism disorders: no keto.
8. Pace faster than about 1% of body weight per week: say so, show the date the safe pace supports, let the dietitian decide.

## B. Diet and religion
**Halal.** No pork or pork derivatives (lard, bacon, ham, pork gelatin). No alcohol, including wine in cooking and alcohol-based vanilla or flavor extracts. Meat from halal-certified or zabiha sources. Check labels on gelatin (yogurt, gummies, softgel capsules), enzymes and rennet (cheese), whey and broth. Shrimp and some seafood differ by school of practice (Hanafi views differ); flag and offer a fish swap. Plan around Ramadan if the plan overlaps it: confirm the start date by moon sighting, offer a suhoor and iftar version with the same macros, no deficit pressure on fasting days, hydration and electrolytes. Check: Ramadan dates for the plan window.
**Kosher.** No pork or shellfish; fish must have fins and scales; no mixing meat and dairy (wait the family's custom, often 6 hours); certified products; no insects in leafy greens. Shabbat meals prepared in advance.
**Vegan.** B12 (supplement is required), iron (with vitamin C, away from tea or coffee), zinc, iodine, calcium, vitamin D, algae omega-3, complete protein across the day. Check: B12 dose in current guidance.
**Vegetarian / pescatarian.** B12 and iron still matter; fish twice a week for omega-3.
**Allergies and refusals.** Hard exclusions in every meal, recipe and swap, including sauces and supplements (peanut, tree nut, sesame, shellfish, soy, gluten, dairy).

## C. Conditions and medications
**Hypothyroidism (levothyroxine).** Take on waking with water only, 30 to 60 minutes before food or coffee. Keep iron, calcium, magnesium, antacids, soy and high-fiber supplements about 4 hours away. Pause biotin 2 to 3 days before TSH tests. Selenium: do not exceed 400 mcg a day from all sources. Iodine adequate (iodized salt), avoid large doses of kelp. Cruciferous vegetables: cooked in normal amounts are usually fine, but follow the client's instruction literally if she avoids them. Soy: allowed, but keep it away from the dose. Strict carb restriction can lower T3 in some people: use refeeds, keep the deficit modest, recheck labs. Labs: TSH, free T4, free T3 at 8 to 12 weeks. Unit check: levothyroxine is mcg, not mg. Check: current ATA guidance on diet and dosing interactions.
**Gout / high uric acid.** Avoid high-purine foods (organ meats, some seafood such as sardines and anchovies), limit alcohol (beer most), avoid fructose-sweetened drinks, hydrate, gradual weight loss. Ketosis and fast weight loss can raise uric acid for a time: avoid keto and long fasts unless the physician agrees. Check: current ACR guideline, purine content of each protein used.
**Type 2 diabetes / prediabetes.** Spread carbs evenly, favor fiber, watch hypoglycemia with sulfonylureas or insulin (see gate 4), coordinate fasting with medication timing, A1c at 3 months. Check: ADA nutrition consensus and the client's own medication list.
**Hypertension.** Sodium about 1,500 to 2,300 mg a day, DASH-style produce and potassium unless on an ACE inhibitor, ARB or potassium-sparing diuretic, or with kidney disease (then do not push potassium or "electrolyte" products). Keto electrolyte advice must not conflict with this: confirm.
**PCOS.** Lower-glycemic carbs, protein at each meal, strength training, modest deficit; check fasting is tolerated.
**High LDL / cardiovascular risk.** Keto and high saturated fat can raise LDL: unsaturated fats first, lipid panel at 8 to 12 weeks.

## D. Approach checks
**Ketogenic days.** Net carbs 20 g or less, fat-forward, protein not excessive, electrolytes (sodium, potassium, magnesium) in the first two weeks, keto flu, constipation (fiber, fluids). Cyclical keto: keto on rest days, higher carbs around training. Check: contraindications above.
**Intermittent fasting (16:8, 19:5).** Medication timing with meals, hypoglycemia, women's cycle effects, training fuel (eat protein within 2 hours after training), break the fast with protein. Not for the gate groups above.
**Carb cycling to training.** Higher carbs on training days only, hard cap as stated by the client, lower and keto days on rest days. Protein and calories steady.
**High protein.** About 1.6 to 2.4 g per kg; check kidney status; spread 35 to 65 g across meals.
**Supplements.** Creatine monohydrate 3 to 5 g, vitamin D by blood level, omega-3 1 to 2 g, magnesium glycinate, iron only if labs are low. All "confirm with physician". Capsule gelatin source for halal and vegan clients.
