# Gout and high uric acid rules used by the engine

These mirror the client's chart (Eat, Limit, Avoid) plus the dietitian's later rule: other foods are fine if they do not raise uric acid.

Allowed daily: egg whites, plain nonfat yogurt, low-fat cottage cheese, part-skim mozzarella, whey protein isolate, low-carb vegetables (kale, zucchini, green beans, broccoli, cucumber, lettuce, tomato, cabbage, celery, radish), olive oil, walnuts, almonds, chia, flax, avocado, berries and cherries, coffee, tea, lemon, herbs and spices, water 3 L or more.

Carb-day foods: oats, quinoa, brown rice, sweet potato, whole-wheat bread, bananas, apples, pears, peaches, pineapple.

Limited (caps used by the engine):
- Whole eggs: 2 or fewer per day.
- Chicken breast: 4 oz per meal, 3 times a week.
- White fish, baked: 6 oz, 2 times a week.
- Salmon 4 oz and tuna 4 oz: once a week each.
- Tofu: 6 oz, 2 times a week.
- Oats: one third cup, about 2 times a week.
- One chicken or fish meal per day, never two.

Avoid entirely: red meat and organ meats, sardines, anchovies, mackerel, herring, trout, shellfish, turkey, duck, goose, game meats, bacon, sausages, hot dogs and processed meats, yeast extracts, meat broths and gravies, beer and all alcohol, soda and sugary drinks, high-fructose corn syrup. Legumes, spinach, asparagus, mushrooms, cauliflower and peanut butter were left out of this plan.

To adapt for another condition (kidney, diabetes, allergy), change the `DB` ingredient list and caps in `plan_engine.py` and the Rules and Foods text in `assets/webapp_template.html`.
