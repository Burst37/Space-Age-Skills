# Carb cycling pattern

Weekly pattern, Monday first (so the start date must be a Monday):
Mon Medium (about 60 g), Tue Low (about 40 g), Wed Keto (under 25 g), Thu High (80 to 100 g), Fri Low, Sat Medium, Sun Keto.

- Keto: eggs, egg whites, cottage cheese, mozzarella, whey, chicken or fish, low-carb vegetables, olive oil, walnuts, avocado. No grains or fruit. Calories come up with olive oil, which has no carbs.
- Low: keto base plus one small fruit or vegetable add-on (berries, cherries, carrots).
- Medium: base plus a carb side at lunch or dinner (quinoa, sweet potato, fruit).
- High: base plus oats or bread at breakfast, grain or sweet potato at lunch and dinner, fruit before training. Put it on the heaviest training day.

Typical calorie targets by tier: keto 1,600, low 1,600, medium 1,650, high 1,750. Protein stays at 170 g or more every day. Fat rises on low-carb days to replace carbohydrate calories. Weekly average carbs about 53 g.

Weekly average protein, carbs and fat for the client in the example: 182 g, 53 g, 73 g at about 1,620 kcal.
