# Meal photo prompts

Use one 4:3 overhead image per recipe key at 2K. Template:

"Overhead editorial food photo, white plate on light oak table, soft daylight: [exact ingredients as in the recipe]. No [anything not in the recipe]."

Rules that avoid mismatches: describe only ingredients that are in the recipe, add "no bread, no rice, no sauce" style exclusions for foods that tempt the image model, and do not add garnish the client cannot eat. Lemon, fresh herbs and pepper are fine for gout clients. Check a contact sheet of all photos against the recipe list before building, because image models add toast, rice or fruit on their own.

Save as KEY.jpg (B1.jpg, MF1.jpg, MN1.jpg, S1.jpg ...), about 2048 px wide. The build script shrinks them to 560 px for the page.
