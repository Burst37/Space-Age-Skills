# Swap rules

Only swap within a category, matched on the macro that defines it:
- Protein for protein, matched on grams of protein.
- Carbs (grains, starch, fruit) for carbs, matched on grams of carbohydrate.
- Vegetables for vegetables, cup for cup (1 medium tomato counts as three quarters of a cup).
- Healthy fat for healthy fat, matched on grams of fat.
Never swap across categories (no eggs for rice, no green beans for tofu).

Amounts are rounded to a practical step (quarter cup, half ounce, whole egg). Chicken, fish and tofu amounts stop at their portion caps, and the option says when that leaves it short.

The page checks each swap against the limits and shows a red warning instead of blocking: more than one chicken or fish meal in a day, weekly caps on chicken, fish, tofu and oats, more than 2 whole eggs a day, and carbs over 25 g on a keto day. Swaps are saved in the viewer's browser only. They update the day totals, calendar and shopping list.
