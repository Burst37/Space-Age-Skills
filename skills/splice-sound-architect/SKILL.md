## QUALITY GATES  
  
Before any Splice sound is used in a deliverable:  
- ✅ Confirm royalty-free license covers commercial use case  
- ✅ BPM matches or is adjusted for target track  
- ✅ Key is compatible with other musical elements  
- ✅ Sound quality: 44.1kHz / 16-bit minimum, 24-bit preferred  
- ✅ Volume normalized before placing in mix (-18 LUFS reference)
