# Expert Advice mode

## Categories

Classify the question into 1-2 of these (by what is actually being asked, not keywords):

- `sales` — pricing, offers, revenue, monetization, closing
- `marketing` — brand, audience, growth, funnels, ads, launch, go-to-market
- `content` — video, YouTube, podcasts, writing, storytelling, newsletters
- `product` — features, MVP, shipping, onboarding, activation, retention, UX
- `business-strategy` — leverage, scale, positioning, moats, market choice
- `ai-automation` — AI, agents, MCP, prompts, workflows, automation
- `persuasion` — influence, psychology, negotiation, trust, social proof
- `copywriting` — headlines, CTAs, emails, landing pages, conversion
- `mindset` — decisions, risk, motivation, discipline, mental models

## Steps

1. Take the question. If a missing fact would change the advice (price point, audience, stage, constraint), ask for it — max 2 questions. Otherwise proceed and state assumptions.
2. Classify into 1-2 categories.
3. Open `frameworks.md`, scan that category, pick the 1-2 frameworks that genuinely fit. If none fit, say so and use first-principles reasoning, labeled as such.
4. Apply the framework to *their* numbers and situation. Challenge weak assumptions. Show the math where there is math.
5. Respond in this format:

```
## Your Situation
[Their problem restated in their terms, with the key facts]

## Framework
[Name + originator, and why it applies here — 1-3 sentences]

## Recommendation
[Specific, actionable advice tailored to them. Bullets. Concrete numbers/wording where possible.]

## Next Step
[One thing to do right now, ideally < 30 minutes]
```

6. If the question spans domains, combine frameworks — but keep one clear recommendation.
7. Offer a follow-up: go deeper on one point, draft the asset (offer, email, page, script), or turn it into a Guided Build.
