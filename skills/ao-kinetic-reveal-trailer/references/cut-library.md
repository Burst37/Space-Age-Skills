## Cut recipes (copy, swap nouns)

**01** Pure graphics: a massive {accent} circle slams onto a {light} field, two black bars wipe across, tiny UI ticks and a barcode strip flicker; the letters {A} and {O} punch in one after another with heavy impact shake.

**02** The {O} becomes a porthole: extreme close-up of her {eye color} eye inside it, glancing up; RGB split flash, then the circle shatters into shards of flat color.

**03** Black frame with an enormous white {WORDMARK}; she sprints in from frame left and power-slides across the baseline of the typography, speed lines and {accent} streaks trailing, shards of the letters kicked up like sparks. Whip-pan out.

**04** Split-screen triptych of flat {c1}/{c2}/{c3} panels; in each panel her silhouette performs a different move in stroboscopic freeze: takeoff, tuck, landing. Huge vertical text {FULL SPEED} scrolls behind the panels.

**05** She leaps through a giant rotating ring of typography reading {SLOGAN}; camera tracks her mid-air spin in slow motion as the letters scatter, then snap-zoom on her confident face.

**06** Hard cut: white editorial card, huge black {JUMP} with a {accent} slash; she vaults over the word itself, palm planted on the J, legs whipping across frame; the word compresses like a spring under her hand and rebounds.

**07** {Light} field with a black diagonal stripe; she back-flips along the stripe in three stroboscopic ghost frames, each ghost tinted a different palette color; giant outlined text {FLIP} rotates 180 degrees in sync with her rotation.

**08** Kinetic type barrage: the words {W1} / {W2} / {W3} / {W4} slam in one per beat with shutter flashes and camera shake, while she slides on her knees across the foreground, jacket flaring, frost particles bursting from her sneakers.

**09** Black frame, giant white wireframe grid tilting in 3D; she runs up the grid like a wall, kicks off, and hangs in the air as everything freezes; a {accent} circle stamps around her frozen pose like a target lock with UI brackets and data ticks.

**10** Freeze releases into a burst: she dives toward camera through layered flat-color panes that shatter one by one like glass shutters, each shattering pane revealing a bigger letter of {A-O}; foreground wipe with her sneaker.

**11** Rapid-fire poster montage: four full-screen graphic posters of her in different action poses (mid-flip, sliding, landing, hands-in-pockets) snap past with hard cuts, each with different bold layout, oversized numbers 01-04, barcodes and slashes.

**12** Hero moment on a clean white cyclorama: she lands a final backflip dead center in slow motion, straightens with hands in pockets, and a shockwave of concentric {accent} rings, wind streaks and shattered typography blasts outward from her landing; brief iconic freeze with her faint confident smile, overexpose to white.

**13** Final identity card: enormous black {WORDMARK} dominates a pale white field with translucent {light-accent} rings, technical arcs, halftone and a rough black circular emblem containing a ghosted {snow-crystal/barcode} motif; she stands relaxed overlapping the letters, wind rippling her jacket; one last {light-accent} pulse sweeps through the typography and a frost-ring flash punctuates the end.

## Closing blocks (append after cut 13, verbatim structure)

**Editing:** extremely aggressive rhythm - hard cuts on every beat, graphic matches, whip pans, snap zooms, stroboscopic freezes, foreground wipes, RGB splits, shutter flashes, impact shakes and speed ramps; every cut must feel compositionally different; typography is always fully readable before the character overlaps it. No weapons, no combat, no fire; all energy comes from motion design, wind, frost, glass and her parkour-style athleticism.

**BGM/SFX:** hard-hitting electronic / drum-heavy future bass with aggressive drops, risers, sub hits and glitch fills locked to every cut; sneaker impacts, whooshes, glass shatters and typography slams are rhythmic sound-design elements. Peak at CUT 12 and end with a cold electronic logo stinger.

**Quality tail:** Premium AAA quality, anime-inspired cinematic rendering, stylish and explosive, strong graphic-design identity, consistent character design, exactly 13 cuts.
