# Prompt cookbook

Real questions sellers ask through the SellerMate MCP, grouped by job. Paste any of
these into Claude (or your MCP client of choice) once connected.

## Find wasted spend

- Which search terms spent more than $50 with zero orders in the last 30 days?
- Show me keywords with 100+ clicks and conversion under 2%.
- Which campaigns have the worst ACOS-to-budget ratio this month?
- Negate the zero-order search terms above $30 spend. *(queued through approvals)*

## Diagnose performance

- Why did ACOS jump last week? Break it down by campaign.
- Compare top-of-search vs rest-of-search performance for my brand campaigns.
- Which hours of the day waste the most spend? Should I daypart?
- What changed in my account in the last 7 days, and did performance move after?

## Grow

- Which products convert well organically but have no campaign yet?
- Where am I losing impression share on my best keywords?
- What does Search Query Performance say buyers search before buying my product?
- Launch a Sponsored Products campaign for ASIN B0XXXXXXX with a $25 daily budget.

## See the whole business

- What's my true TACoS this month vs last?
- Give me a one-paragraph marketplace summary I can paste into our Monday standup.
- How is my vendor line performing vs my seller line?

## Meta

- What can you actually do on my account? *(the server describes itself)*
- What did you change yesterday, and under whose approval?
