## Review before integrating

Review the visible action in each shot at normal playback speed. Reject footage when a camera move carries an otherwise static shot, the action takes more than half a second to start, the central participants stand or sit idle, or the physical state of a prop jumps between angles. Confirm mobile center crops show the same action. Keep every clip muted and deliver a matching poster for reduced-motion mode.
